# STACK CHECK
### A 10 episode series for @samiam.in.tech

Built from real scored data in `docs/setups/`. Every hook is drawn from
`hook-library.md` and cited by number. Every number is one you can defend on
camera.

---

## The series promise

> I score AI setups out of 100. Yours is probably worse than you think. Mine was.

One recurring format, one recurring graphic, one running score. The audience is
technical founders and PMs who already use these tools and suspect their setup is
held together with hope.

## The engine: no single episode contains the whole rubric

This is the mechanic that drives profile clicks, so treat it as a rule rather
than a preference.

There are seven dimensions. **Never show all seven in one video.** Each episode
reveals one or two and forward-references the rest by episode number. A viewer
who wants the complete picture cannot get it from the video they landed on. The
only place the full rubric exists is across the profile grid.

That converts a passive viewer into someone with an errand. Curiosity that a
single video can resolve produces a like. Curiosity that only the profile can
resolve produces a profile visit.

Three supporting devices:

1. **Number every episode onscreen.** `STACK CHECK 03` top left, same position,
   every time. Numbering creates a completion drive, and it makes the grid
   legible as a series instead of a pile of videos.
2. **Close on a forward loop, not a summary.** Every episode ends by naming a
   specific unresolved thing and the episode it lives in. "The dimension I scored
   a zero on is 08."
3. **Open with a backreference sometimes.** "If you saw 02 you know I scored a
   37.5." A new viewer has to go look. That is the point.

## Pin these three

| 1 | **02** | Credibility. You failed your own rubric. |
| 2 | **04** | The rubric itself. The thing people came to find. |
| 3 | **09** | The arc. Gives a new follower a reason to stay. |

## Beat map

Applies to every episode. The hook rules hold regardless of length: the decision
happens at 1.5 seconds, so the overlay, the spoken line, and the visual all fire
at once.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Hook. Overlay + spoken + visual, simultaneous. 10 to 14 words. |
| 0:03 to 0:06 | The tension. One line that raises the stakes. |
| 0:06 to 0:40 | Payoff. The numbers, the diagnosis, the reveal. |
| 0:40 to 0:50 | Conviction close, then the forward loop to another episode. |

## Posting schedule

Two per week, Tuesday and Thursday. Six weeks total.

Episodes 01 and 02 go out within 48 hours of each other. The rubric means nothing
until you have failed it publicly, and the failure is what earns the right to
score anyone else.

## After episode 10

The series is designed to keep running without new writing:

- **Scored submissions.** Comment volume from 10 becomes episodes. Same format,
  someone else's stack. Result First hooks (#7 to #12) fit these best.
- **Fix episodes.** Each of the four changes from 09 is its own build video, with
  the scoreboard graphic ticking up. Mid-Action hooks (#37 to #42) fit these.
- **Rescoring.** Rescore your friend after he sends the context files. His
  Curiosity Gap is already loaded: does the best-looking setup hold up.

## What to watch

**Profile visit rate is the metric for this series**, not views. That is the goal
and it is measurable in analytics. If views climb and profile visits don't, the
forward loops are too weak. Make them more specific: name the episode number and
the exact thing withheld.

Expect **03 and 08** to outperform. 03 has a built in reversal. 08 has an
argument people want to fight about in the comments.

If **06** lands, governance becomes your lane and no other tech creator can
follow you into it.

**A/B test worth running:** post 03 with the Specific Number hook, then four
weeks later post the same content with Contrarian #3 adapted ("Setup scores are
backwards. The guy who beat me is in worse shape."). The delta tells you whether
this audience is here for receipts or for takes.

**The signal that matters for the product.** Three or more inbound "score mine"
requests means Phase 2 in `docs/product/next-steps.md` has users waiting. If the
videos land but nobody asks to be scored, the format works and the product
doesn't. Better to learn that before building anything.
