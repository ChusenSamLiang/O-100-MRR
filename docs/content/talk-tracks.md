# Talk Tracks : FLEX-7 Launch Sequence

Eight spoken scripts for @samiam.in.tech, built from real scored data in
`docs/setups/`. Every number in here is one you can defend on camera.

**Voice rules applied:** no em dashes, sentences under 12 words, point first,
conviction closes, one exclamation point maximum across the whole set.

**Pillar:** Tool-first builds + AI economy takes + build in public.

**Run order matters.** Track 1 establishes the rubric exists. Track 2 makes you
credible by failing it. Everything after that has permission to be opinionated.

**Length:** each runs 45 to 60 seconds at natural pace. Roughly 120 to 140 words.

## Production notes

**Posting cadence.** Tracks 1 and 2 within 48 hours of each other. The rubric
means nothing until you've failed it publicly. Then 3 through 7 across two weeks.
Track 8 closes the arc and opens the series.

**The recurring graphic.** Three numbers, always the same layout:
`CONTEXT / FLEXIBILITY / SCALABILITY`. Use it in every video. It becomes the
thing people screenshot, and it's what makes a scored setup shareable later when
the app ships.

**What to watch.** Track 3 and Track 7 should outperform. Track 3 has the
built-in reversal, Track 7 has an argument people want to fight about. If Track 5
lands, governance becomes your lane and nobody can follow you into it.

**The signal that matters.** Three or more inbound "score mine" requests means
Phase 2 in `docs/product/next-steps.md` has users waiting. If nobody asks to be
scored, the format is working and the product isn't. Different problem, and worth
knowing before you build anything.

**Reusable hook structure**, for scoring other people's setups later:

1. State the score flat. No windup.
2. Describe what they run, one layer at a time.
3. Give the flexibility and scalability split. The gap is the story.
4. Name the single fix worth the most points.
5. Close on conviction.
