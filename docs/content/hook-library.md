## The rules behind all of these

- **1.5 seconds is the real decision point.** Meta's creative benchmarks show the largest retention drop happens before 1.5s. 90% of underperforming TikToks fail inside the first 3 seconds.
- **Spoken hook = 10 to 14 words max.** That is what fits in 3 seconds of natural speech.
- **Stack the hook.** Text overlay + spoken line + visual, all firing at once. Highest performers do all three.
- **Never introduce yourself.** "Hey guys" hooks convert at roughly 0.3%. The viewer scrolled to you.
- **Specificity is the credibility.** "34,635 clips" beats "a lot of clips." Named tools beat "AI tools."
- **Raw beats polished.** Authentic-looking content outperforms produced content by roughly 60%.

Highest average views by hook type: **Result First** (Outcome Showcase) at ~6,037 avg views, roughly 2x the lowest-ranked type. The four patterns that consistently score highest across 2026 datasets: **Identity Call, Contrarian, Open Loop, Confession.**

## 1. CONTRARIAN / TAKEDOWN
*Top-4 scoring pattern. Creates unresolved cognitive tension. Your strongest lane given the "skeptical of hype" voice.*

1. "Everyone's wrong about AI replacing engineers. It's replacing something else."
2. "Stop learning prompt engineering. It's already a dead skill."
3. "The AI bubble talk is backwards. Here's what's actually overvalued."
4. "Cursor isn't better than Claude Code. People just don't know how to use one of them."
5. "Your company doesn't need an AI strategy. It needs to fire its AI committee."
6. "Vibe coding isn't lazy. The people saying that haven't shipped anything this year."

**Formula:** [Widely held belief]. It's wrong. / Here's what's actually true.

## 2. RESULT FIRST / OUTCOME SHOWCASE
*Highest average views of any hook type. Show the finished thing in frame one, explain after.*

7. "I built this entire app on a 45-minute subway ride. Here's the commit log."
8. "This shipped to production 20 minutes ago. I wrote none of the code."
9. "This dashboard cost me $4 in tokens and replaced a $600/month tool."
10. "Three MCP servers. One prompt. This is what came out."
11. "My Claude Code session just refactored 11,000 lines while I was on the L train."
12. "This is what a one-person startup's entire stack looks like in 2026."

**Formula:** [Finished result on screen] + [the constraint that makes it impressive].

## 3. CURIOSITY GAP / OPEN LOOP
*Top-4 scoring pattern. Reveal the outcome, withhold the mechanism. Zeigarnik effect: the brain needs to close the loop.*

13. "There's a setting in Claude Code that nobody turns on. It changes everything about long sessions."
14. "I found out why my agent kept failing at 3am. It wasn't the model."
15. "Anthropic shipped something last week and almost nobody noticed what it actually unlocks."
16. "The reason your AI code review is useless has nothing to do with the AI."
17. "I deleted 80% of my prompt and it got better. Here's the part that was hurting it."
18. "One line in my CLAUDE.md file cut my token spend in half."

**Formula:** [Surprising outcome] + [the cause is not what you think].

## 4. IDENTITY CALL
*Top-4 scoring pattern. Filters hard, which is the point. The right viewer feels named.*

19. "If you're a technical founder still hiring engineers the old way, this is going to sting."
20. "If you've opened Claude Code and closed it in under five minutes, you did one thing wrong."
21. "PMs who can't ship a prototype themselves: you have about 18 months."
22. "If your AI startup's moat is a wrapper, watch this before your next raise."
23. "Anyone building with agents in production knows this failure mode. Nobody documents it."
24. "You're a solo founder with no budget. Here's the stack I'd run if I started over today."

**Formula:** If you're [specific identity] doing [specific thing], [consequence or promise].

## 5. SPECIFIC NUMBER / RECEIPTS
*Precision signals credibility. Odd, non-round numbers outperform round ones.*

26. "$1,847 in API spend last month. Here's the line item that was pure waste."
27. "I tested 12 AI coding tools. Only 3 survived a real codebase."
28. "47 minutes. One train ride. A working MVP. Here's the tape."
29. "We cut our eng team's ticket cycle from 6 days to 11 hours. Two changes did it."
30. "I read all 74 pages of the model card so you don't have to. Three things matter."

**Formula:** [Odd specific number] + [what I extracted from it].

## 6. CONFESSION / MISTAKE WARNING
*Top-4 scoring pattern. Vulnerability buys attention, and the warning framing triggers loss aversion.*

31. "I wasted four months building the wrong thing because I trusted the model's plan."
32. "I let an agent touch prod. I want to tell you exactly what happened."
33. "I've been using AI wrong for a year and a half. Nobody corrected me."
34. "My biggest mistake as a technical founder was thinking I could out-build the hiring problem."
35. "I burned $900 in tokens in one night. Here's the loop I didn't catch."
36. "I told my team AI would make us faster. Six weeks later I had to walk it back."

**Formula:** I [did the wrong thing] for [duration/cost]. Here's what I'd do instead.

## 7. MID-ACTION / IN MEDIA RES
*Start mid-sentence, mid-build, mid-reaction. The absence of setup is the hook.*

37. "...and that's when I realized the agent had been lying about the tests passing."
38. "Okay it just did the thing. Watch the terminal."
39. "No no no. Look at what it just wrote."
40. "I'm on the train, I have 40 minutes, and this thing has to ship before Union Square."
41. "This is minute 22 of a build I said would take 10."
42. "Rerunning it right now. If this works I'm never writing boilerplate again."

**Formula:** Drop the viewer into a moment already in progress. No context. Context comes at second 5.

45. "Rating AI startup pitch decks by how many times they say 'agentic' before slide 4."

48. "Every AI product demo is a guy with an unfair internet connection and one good prompt."

**Formula:** [Universal builder pain] delivered flat, no punchline signaling.

## 10. IMPERATIVE COMMAND
*High risk, high reward. Direct interruption. Use sparingly so it keeps its force.*

55. "Stop scrolling if you've ever had an agent silently break your build."
56. "Before you pay for another AI subscription, check this one thing."
57. "Don't start that project until you've written this file."
58. "Open your token usage dashboard right now. I'll wait."
59. "Delete your prompt library. I'm serious. Here's what to do instead."
60. "Watch this before your next sprint planning. It'll save you a week."

**Formula:** [Direct command] + [immediate stake for ignoring it].

## How to deploy these

**Rotate types, never repeat one twice in a row.** Pattern fatigue is real and your regulars will start scrolling on sight.

**Match hook type to content pillar:**
| Pillar | Best hook types |
|---|---|
| AI industry news | News Break, Contrarian, Curiosity Gap |
| Tool-first builds | Result First, Mid-Action, Specific Number |
| Founder / PM life | Confession, Identity Call, Humor |
| AI economy takes | Contrarian, Identity Call, News Break |
| Subway Builds | Result First, Mid-Action, Specific Number |

**A/B test the same content with two hooks.** Post the same build with a Result First hook and a Confession hook one week apart. The delta tells you what your audience is actually there for.

**Structure after the hook:**
- 0 to 3s: hook (overlay + spoken + visual)
- 3 to 5s: the tension or setup
- 6 to 12s: the payoff, reveal, or insight
- 13 to 15s: CTA

**CTAs that match your voice:** "Follow if you're building something." / "Save this, you'll need it when you hit this problem." / "Drop your stack in the comments." / "Don't get filtered. Build the thing."
