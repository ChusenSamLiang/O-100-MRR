# 30-Day Production Package

**Channel:** @samiam.in.tech
**21 posts, 30 days, 3 lanes.** Video only. No image posts. No news pegs.
**Lanes:** A Consequence (AI x jobs x money) · C Subway Build · D Receipts and Failures

Everything you need to shoot and publish each post: capture note, cold open, live beats, close, on-screen text, caption, tags.

---

## How to use this

Each entry gives you five things. Two are verbatim, three are not.

- **Claim** and **Close** are verbatim. Memorize exactly these two sentences. Nothing else.
- **Beats** are bullets you improvise around. Do not write them into sentences.
- **On-screen text** goes in the editor, not the shoot.
- **Caption** is written the way people search, not the way you brand.

Every `[X]` is a slot for a real number from your own logs, invoice, or calendar. Never invent one. If you don't have the number, swap the concept.

---

## Production rhythm

Batch capture on weekends, edit and post on weekdays. That's what makes 5 posts a week survivable while running a startup.

| Block | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Capture block 1** | Sat/Sun, days 6–7 | Shoot week 2 lanes A and D. Two hours, one setup. |
| **Capture block 2** | Sat/Sun, days 13–14 | Shoot week 3 lanes A and D. |
| **Capture block 3** | Sat/Sun, days 20–21 | Shoot week 4 lanes A and D. |
| **Subway Builds** | Live, during commute | Never batched. The commute is the shoot. |
| **Receipts** | Opportunistic | Keep a capture running during real work. Harvest, don't stage. |

Post at the same time every day. Pull the time from your own analytics, not from a blog.

---

## The repeated structure

Same on every one of the 21. This repetition is the continuity.

**Cold open**
1. Artifact on screen, 0 to 1s. No face, no "so." Published frame one is roughly recorded second eight.
2. **Claim**, 1 to 4s, verbatim.
3. **Turn**, 4 to 7s. Opens the gap.

**Middle** runs live, 40 to 150s.

**Close**, verbatim, then the tag: **"Build the thing."** Never vary the tag.

**Per-lane first-line grammar**

| Lane | Grammar |
|---|---|
| A | [Concrete fact from your team]. [What it actually means]. |
| C | Subway Build [N]. [Time], [constraint], [what shipped]. |
| D | [Number]. [What it bought or cost]. |

**Target length:** 60 to 180 seconds. Say the caption keyword out loud in the first two seconds.

## Day 30 · Review, no post

Pull the numbers. Fill this in.

| Lane | Posts | Median views | Avg completion | Best post | Worst post |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Consequence | 7 | | | | |
| C Subway Build | 7 | | | | |
| D Receipts | 7 | | | | |

**Read it this way:**

- **Median, not mean.** One breakout hides a weak lane. Use the middle post.
- **Completion is the truth signal.** High views with low completion means the hook worked and the content didn't. Fix the middle, keep the lane.
- **Low views with high completion** means the content is good and discovery failed. Keep the lane, rewrite captions as queries.
- **Both low** kills the lane.

**Then:** kill the weakest lane, double the strongest, and keep Subway Build running regardless of where it ranks. The series is the spine, not a test variable.

## Standing rules

1. Two memorized sentences per video. Claim and Close. Everything else live.
2. Published frame one is roughly recorded second eight. Cut the ramp-in.
3. Never open on your face saying "so."
4. Say the caption keyword out loud in the first two seconds.
5. 60 to 180 seconds.
6. Same tag every time: "Build the thing."
7. Never fabricate a number. Swap the concept instead.
